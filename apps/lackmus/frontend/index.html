Glossary of Terms

About Financial Compass

Financial Compass is a free web tool for financial statement analysis. Enter balance sheet and income statement figures, or upload a PDF or image, and it computes liquidity, leverage, profitability, activity and market ratios, a health score, a DuPont decomposition and charts.

Ratios covered

Current ratio, acid-test ratio, cash ratio, debt-to-equity, debt ratio, interest coverage, gross/operating/net margin, ROE, ROA, asset, receivables and inventory turnover, and price-to-earnings. Each ratio has a formula and a plain-language reading.

What is the current ratio and what is a good value?

Current ratio = current assets / current liabilities. Values around 1.5 or higher are usually considered comfortable; below 1 signals liquidity risk.

What does the DuPont analysis show?

It splits return on equity (ROE) into net profit margin x asset turnover x financial leverage, showing whether ROE comes from profitability, efficiency or debt.

Is my financial data stored?

Ratios are calculated in your browser. Documents uploaded for extraction are processed by the server and a language model only to read figures; the arithmetic is plain code.

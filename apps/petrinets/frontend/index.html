About the Hotel Pricing Simulator

A free tool that simulates hotel booking demand with a Petri net and recommends a room price. Set room count, demand and cancellation behaviour, then see occupancy over time and a transparent price recommendation.

Method

Stochastic Petri-net simulation with places for available, pending and confirmed rooms, repeated as a Monte Carlo experiment; price interpolates between a floor and a ceiling according to expected occupancy.

How does the pricing simulator work?

A Petri net models booking demand: rooms move between available, pending and confirmed places as bookings arrive and cancel. Many Monte Carlo runs give expected occupancy, and the price is interpolated within a set band from that occupancy.

Does the AI set the price?

No. Simulation and price interpolation are deterministic code. A language model only writes the plain-language rationale.

Why a Petri net for hotel bookings?

Petri nets make resource states and transitions explicit, so token conservation (no room is created or lost) and each firing rule can be checked.

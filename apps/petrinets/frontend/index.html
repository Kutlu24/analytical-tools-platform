Petrinets

A Petri-net room-pricing simulator

For small, independent hotels. Enter your current situation, get a transparent price suggestion grounded in a real stochastic simulation of booking demand - not a black box.

This tool has no historical booking or pricing data behind it - the demand-model constants are documented, reasonable assumptions, not fitted to any real hotel's outcomes (see the project README). Treat the output as a transparent starting point for your own judgment, not a certified optimal price.
